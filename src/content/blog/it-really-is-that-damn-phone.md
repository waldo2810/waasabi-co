title: It really is that damn phone
date: 2026-06-28
description: It really is that damn phone
---

It's common for me to ask myself: why don't I have time to read? or for hobbies? For quite some time now, I feel like I don't have time such things. Wekends come, and I don't have time to do "stuff." I feel like I don't want to do things on the first place. Because I'm afraid of being tired. Weekends go, and didn't have time to rest. There is a pattern here. <u>It really is that damn phone</u>. It's the phone and its app making me waste my time. Tiktok, Instagram, Twitter, Discord, Reddit. All meticulously designed to keep you *hooked*. To make you feel the need of staying a little more. You're presented and bombarded with vast amounts of content that feel specifically targeted at you. You feel dependent of them. Time flies. Flies faster when you browse them and you always feel that, maybe, the next post will be even better. They make you enter in this form of 'alternate reality', in which, your brain is either constantly reacting to short-form 60-second content, reflecting on the past or planning about the future; but never help you live your present. They make you ask yourself whether you should turn off the screen and go live. That, nothing you're seeing is happening to you right now and, actually, you're at your bed wasting your time to do "stuff."
